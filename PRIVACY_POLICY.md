## 1. Overview

C++ Studio is a C++ integrated development environment (IDE) for Windows. This policy explains how the app handles your data. In short: **the app does not collect any personal information, does not transmit data anywhere, and does not share anything with third parties.**

## 2. Data we do not collect

C++ Studio:

- does **not** ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, or any other personal information;
- does **not** require registration or an account;
- does **not** send telemetry, analytics, or usage statistics;
- does **not** display ads or use advertising identifiers;
- does **not** access your location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

## 3. Internet connection

The app does not require an internet connection and does **not send or receive any data over the network** during normal operation. The compiler (g++) and libraries are bundled with the app and run locally on your computer.

## 4. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally, only on your device, in the `data` folder inside the installation directory:

- app settings (theme, language, font size, compiler options, window position);
- the list of recently opened folders and files.

This data **never leaves your computer**. You can delete it at any time by removing the `data` folder or uninstalling the app.

## 5. Your files and code

The code and files you write or open stay on your device. The app uses them locally to edit and compile; it does not copy or transmit them. Temporary files may be created during compilation and remain on your device.

## 6. Third-party components

The app uses open-source components (for example, the GNU toolchain based on MSYS2/MinGW-w64, and the Qt/PySide6 library). They run locally inside the app and, to our knowledge, are not used to collect or transmit data. They are distributed under their own licenses.

## 7. Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will change. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated beforehand.
